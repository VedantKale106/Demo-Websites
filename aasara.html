Our Story

A Place That Feels
Like Coming Home

Aasara Lunch Home has been a trusted name on the Pune-Nashik Highway in Chakan, Maharashtra. We believe in simple food made well — fresh seafood, slow-cooked meats, and wholesome thalis that honour the spirit of Maharashtrian home cooking.

Whether you are a regular commuter stopping for a quick lunch or a family looking for a satisfying meal, our kitchen is always ready to serve you with warmth and care.
