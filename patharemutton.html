About Us

A Tradition of Taste in Every Bite.

Welcome to Pathare Mutton Khanawal, a down-to-earth restaurant nestled near Woodsville Phase 3, Moshi. We pride ourselves on serving the most authentic Maharashtrian meat preparations, exactly the way they were meant to be enjoyed.

Whether you are here for our legendary Chicken Raan, rich Mutton Sukka, or a fulfilling Thali meal served on traditional banana leaves, we promise an experience that celebrates local culinary heritage. With carefully separated Family and Bar sections, we maintain a beautiful, hygienic ambiance suitable for everyone.

  • Authentic Maharashtrian Recipes
  • Dedicated Family & Bar Sections
  • Meals served traditionally on banana leaves

Chicken Raan

Signature

Special Chicken Thali
