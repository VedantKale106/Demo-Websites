Our Story

A Culinary Landmark Near Khed Ghat

Strategically located on National Highway 50 in Rajgurunagar, Akash Misal House has established itself as a premier destination for travelers and locals alike. We are committed to serving the highest quality vegetarian cuisine in a clean, welcoming environment.

Whether you are stopping by for an early morning breakfast of hot Kande Pohe, indulging in our famous Akash Special Cheese Misal for lunch, or enjoying authentic South Indian delicacies, our kitchen prioritizes fresh ingredients and authentic Maharashtrian spices.

Beyond great food, we understand the needs of modern travelers. Our facility features ample parking, exceptionally clean washrooms, and dedicated EV charging stations, ensuring a comfortable rest stop for your journey.

Discover Our Flavors

Popular Menu Highlights

From our signature Misal to authentic South Indian classics, every dish is prepared with fresh ingredients and traditional recipes.

Maharashtrian Specials

Akash Special Cheese Misal

Popular

Our signature spicy sprouted moth bean curry topped with farsan, onions, coriander, and generous grated cheese. Served with pav.

Kothimbir Vadi

Crispy, savory cakes made from fresh coriander leaves, chickpea flour, and traditional spices. A perfect starter.

Kande Pohe

Classic Maharashtrian breakfast of flattened rice roasted with onions, mustard seeds, turmeric, and peanuts. Light and flavourful.

Chura Masala Papad

Roasted papad crushed and mixed with finely chopped onions, tomatoes, coriander, and chat masala.

South Indian Classics

Masala Dosa

Thin, crisp rice and lentil crepe stuffed with a spiced potato mash. Served with fresh coconut chutney and hot sambar.

Cheese Dosa

A fusion twist on the classic plain dosa, cooked to perfection and loaded with melted cheddar cheese.

Idli Sambar

Soft, fluffy steamed rice cakes served submerged in a bowl of hot, flavorful lentil vegetable stew.

Mixed Veg Uttapam

Thick, savory pancake made from dosa batter, topped with finely diced onions, tomatoes, chilies, and coriander.

Sweets & Beverages

Jalebi Rabdi

Special

Hot, crispy jalebis dipped in saffron syrup, served with thick, creamy chilled rabdi. The perfect contrast.

Special Masala Tea

Strong Indian tea brewed fresh with milk, cardamom, ginger, and our secret mix of spices.

Also available: Tandoor Roti, Vada, various Thalis, Fresh Juices, and Buttermilk.
