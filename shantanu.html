Our Story

A Garden of Flavours,
A Home Away from Home

Hotel Shantanu Garden & Family Restaurant has been a cherished dining landmark in Chakan for over a decade. Nestled near Vishal Garden on the Shikarpur Highway, our restaurant combines the warmth of home cooking with the quality of a fine dining experience.

From sizzling Tandoori specialities to rich Malwani curries and crispy Dosas, our kitchen celebrates the diverse culinary heritage of Maharashtra and beyond. Whether you dine in our lush garden or our air-conditioned dining hall, every meal is crafted with care.
